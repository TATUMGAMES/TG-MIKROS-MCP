# Sample prompts: Launch preparation

## Prompts

- Prepare my game for launch.
- Get my game ready to ship.
- What do I need before launching on the App Store?
- Help me launch my indie game.

## Expected tool flow

See [WORKFLOWS.md](../WORKFLOWS.md#1-prepare-my-game-for-launch).

1. Authenticate
2. `create_project` or `list_projects`
3. `generate_api_keys` (dev)
4. SDK integration guidance
5. `recommend_events`
6. `validate_installation`
7. Launch checklist (summary)
8. `create_campaign` (draft only)
9. Community / influencer suggestions

## Example assistant response (opening)

> I'll walk you through a full launch prep workflow: project setup, analytics, event recommendations, validation, a launch checklist, and an optional marketing campaign draft. We'll use Development keys until everything is verified.
